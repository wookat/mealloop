# MealLoop — brand story & positioning

## One-line positioning
**MealLoop is the calm way for busy families to answer "what's for dinner?" — plan the week in minutes, and everyone shops from one always-in-sync list.**

## The story
Every family has the same 6pm problem: someone standing in front of the fridge, tired, deciding dinner from scratch — again. Recipes live in ten browser tabs, the shopping list is a text thread, and whoever's at the store buys the wrong things twice.

MealLoop closes the loop. You save recipes once (paste any URL, type them in, or import a backup), drag them onto a week, and the grocery list writes itself — merged quantities, sorted by aisle, minus what's already in your pantry. Your household shares one link: anyone can check things off at the store and everyone else sees it instantly. When the week's done, you loop: reuse a saved menu, let AI draft the next week from your own recipe box, and keep cooking the food your family actually eats.

The name is the promise: **meals, on a loop** — plan → shop → cook → repeat, without the nightly decision fatigue.

## Who it's for
Primary: the household "meal captain" in a busy family (parents of school-age kids, dual-income couples) who plans for 2–6 people, cooks 4–7 nights a week, and shares shopping duty.

Secondary: couples and flatmates splitting groceries; anyone escaping a bloated recipe app.

## What we believe (brand pillars)
1. **Calm over clever.** No forced onboarding quizzes, no gamification, no notification spam. The product gets out of the way of dinner.
2. **The family is the unit, not the user.** Households, shared lists, anonymous share links — no one is forced to make an account to check off milk.
3. **Your food, your data.** Recipes export in an open format; accounts self-delete; analytics are cookie-free aggregates. No ads, ever.
4. **Warmth, not sterility.** Cream backgrounds, appetizing warmth, small delightful moments (a checked-off list celebrates) — food software should feel like a kitchen, not a spreadsheet.

## Differentiation (vs. field)
- vs **Samsung Food / SideChef**: no 6-step signup quiz, no ad-cluttered recipe pages, no data harvesting.
- vs **Paprika / Crouton / Mela** (single-user apps): built household-first with real-time shared lists and anonymous share links.
- vs **Plan to Eat**: simpler day-one experience, pantry-aware grocery generation, AI weekly drafts from *your own* recipe box.
- vs spreadsheets/notes: structured recipes, quantity merging, aisle sorting, calendar feeds — with note-level simplicity.

## Proof points (keep current)
- Import from any recipe site (JSON-LD) + manual + JSON backup import/export.
- One-click weekly grocery list: merged quantities, aisle categories, staples, pantry skip.
- Real-time household sync + anonymous share link + iCal calendar feed.
- AI week drafts (glm) grounded in the household's own recipe box.
- Privacy-first: no ads, no cookies before login, cookie-free aggregate analytics, GDPR self-serve erasure.
- Open beta: full product free during beta; published pricing (Free / Household $3/mo · $24/yr / Supporter $29/yr) takes effect at GA.
